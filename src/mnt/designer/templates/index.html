    A quick tour of your workspace

    1. Start with your logic

      Write Verilog, import a .v file, or choose Try an example. The editor saves your code to the current session.

    2. Make it a layout

      Choose Orthogonal for a first layout, or explore the parameters in Exact and GOLD. You can also create a blank layout and place gates yourself.

    3. Refine, verify, export

      Optimize the placement, check design rules, and compare the circuit with your Verilog. Export an .fgl file to reopen your work later, or choose a visualization format.

    Find your way around

    Select a gate or action in the toolbar, then follow the instructions below the workspace. Drag the canvas background to pan and scroll to zoom. When the canvas is focused, use the arrow keys to pan. Press Esc to cancel the current action.

    The four tile shades show the clock phases, progressing from 1 through 4. Use the fit button to bring the entire layout back into view.
